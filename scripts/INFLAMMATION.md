# Inflammation quick check (voice meal logging)

Seed oils are a 👎 on their own, no matter the carbs (`--trigger "seed oils"`).

## Seed oils to flag
- Soybean
- Canola (rapeseed)
- Corn
- Sunflower
- Safflower
- Grapeseed
- Rice bran
- Cottonseed
- "Vegetable oil" and vegetable oil blends

## Where they hide
- Restaurant and fast-food frying (fries, nuggets, fried fish, chips)
- Packaged snacks and crackers
- Dressings, sauces and mayo
- Margarine and spreads
- Peanut butter (unless it's just peanuts and salt)
- Protein bars and granola
- Bread and baked goods
- Frozen meals
- Labels that say "vegetable oil" or "natural flavors"

When you can't tell, assume restaurant fried food and packaged foods have seed oils.

## Other big inflammation contributors
Watch for added sugar, refined flour, processed meat, deep-fried food and alcohol. Mention them in the verdict reason, but only seed oils trigger a 👎 on their own.

## Key experts
The verdicts draw on Dr. Ken Berry, Dr. Jason Fung and Dr. Eric Berg. They help explain what is good to eat, but they never override the meal rules. A meal still gets a thumbs down for any seed oil, more than 10 g of carbs, or a third meal that day. General nutrition is the third factor you asked for, but it is not defined yet, so it does not affect the verdict.
